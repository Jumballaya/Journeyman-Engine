# What the engine lacked (Neon Vow)

Written as the demo was built; each entry says how it was resolved.

- **AssemblyScript doesn't narrow module-level nullables.** `const spawn = map.object("spawn")` at the top of a script, then `if (spawn !== null) spawn.x` fails to compile; `spawn!.x` works. A language rule, not the engine's: noted for AGENTS.md's gotchas.
- **A collider's layer was also what ground held.** Sentries on their own layer (`layerMask: 4`, to tell them apart) fell straight through the level's ground, then on layer 1 only, and so never met Kage: no error, just enemies gone. Fixed in the engine: ground now holds every layer unless a `GroundComponent` narrows its `layerMask`. The sentries are told apart by their tag.

- **Move-driven supports report zero takeoff velocity.** This is documented behavior (`platformVelocityX/Y` describe velocity-driven moving platforms). The rail must use `Path.at()` plus `move()` for platform carry, so the cart publishes its measured per-frame x/y velocity and Kage inherits it on a jump. Horizontal drift lasts until landing; carrying and landing still use the engine's solid collider/support system. No engine change needed.
- **Exact checkpoint placement on a rising terrain sample can snag the box.** At the first station layout, deep-linking to x=5240 put the 32-unit-wide collider partly into the neighboring uphill sample. Walking stopped at x=5243.99 with `blockedX=1`, `blockedY=-1`; `near` reported only Map terrain. Normal traversal onto that slope worked. The station now has a genuinely level boarding pad (x=5220–5380), so both deep links and life-loss respawns start clear of the uphill edge. No engine edit.
- **Scripts can't read the physics step.** `onUpdate` gets dt up to 0.1 s, but physics2d integrates at most 1/20 s a frame (`simulationStep`). A `move()`-driven sled on the raw dt outran a rider flying on physics velocity below 20 fps. Fix: `lib/cart.ts` mirrors the cap in `physicsStep()`. A runtime accessor would remove the duplicated constant.
